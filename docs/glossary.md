# Glossary

**mode.** An oscillator carrying a pair of quadratures.

**quadrature.** A position-like or momentum-like observable, or their phase-space rotation.

**cluster state.** An entangled CV resource usually prepared from squeezed modes and CZ interactions.

**graph state.** A resource whose labelled weighted graph describes entangling connectivity.

**resource state.** An ancilla prepared to enable a measurement-based operation.

**measurement pattern.** An ordered causal sequence of preparations, gates, measurements and corrections.

**feed-forward.** Classical use of earlier outcomes to choose later operations.

**flow.** A structural notion of correction support and measurement order; specify which definition is used.

**CV flow.** Here, a supplied-order real-linear correction certificate; not qubit GF(2) gflow.

**homodyne.** Measurement of one rotated quadrature.

**heterodyne.** Joint noisy phase-space measurement returning two coordinates.

**PNR.** Photon-number-resolving detection with integer outcomes.

**finite squeezing.** A physical finite-energy resource, with nonzero residual quadrature noise.

**Fock cutoff.** Numerical truncation retaining occupations with total photon number below c.

**conditional trajectory.** One complete evolution conditioned on its measurement records.

**postselection.** Selecting a specified measurement branch and retaining its normalized state.

**Gaussian.** A state with Gaussian phase-space characteristic function, or an operation preserving Gaussianity.

**non-Gaussian.** A state or operation outside the Gaussian family.

**cubic phase.** The nonlinear phase exp(i gamma q³/6) in this package.

**Kerr.** The number-dependent phase exp(i kappa n²).

**Wigner function.** A phase-space quasiprobability; negative values can diagnose nonclassical states.

**GKP.** An oscillator encoding using a phase-space lattice; numerical resources have finite energy.

**automatic differentiation.** Propagation of derivatives through implemented numerical operations; sampling needs an estimator contract.
