# Code of conduct

PhotoGraphiQ welcomes participation regardless of age, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Treat participants with respect. Discuss evidence and ideas constructively,
accept corrections, acknowledge mistakes, respect differing experiences, and
consider the effect of your words on the community.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, personal
attacks, and publication of another person's private information without consent
are unacceptable. These standards apply in project spaces and when representing
the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's reporting tools to flag abusive content. For confidential reports,
contact GitHub Support rather than posting personal information publicly.
Maintainers may remove inappropriate contributions or restrict participation.
Reports should be handled with care for the privacy of everyone involved.

Responses should be proportionate: a correction for a minor incident, a warning
for repeated behavior, a temporary restriction for serious misconduct, or a
permanent ban for sustained harassment or threats. Maintainers should explain
decisions where doing so does not compromise privacy or safety.

This project-specific policy follows the inclusion and proportionate-enforcement
principles of the Contributor Covenant. It is not presented as an unmodified copy
of that document.
