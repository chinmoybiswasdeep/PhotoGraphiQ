## What NOT to claim

- R17 (Graphix) validates **structure only** -- never cite it as evidence
  for CV amplitude, quadrature or Wigner-function correctness.
- R23/R25/R26-R28 are finite-resource or finite-energy **approximations**;
  do not present their fidelities as exact-gate or fault-tolerant results.
- R36-R38 timings are single-machine observations; do not present the
  overhead ratio in R38 as a general claim about Piquasso's or
  PhotoGraphiQ's performance on other hardware.
- No experiment in this suite claims a GKP fault-tolerance threshold,
  a universal non-Gaussian compiler, or an optimal/general CV-flow finder.
